Design hex maps for the MakaMek tabletop wargame with an intuitive graphical interface:

- Edit terrain types: clear, woods, rough, water and more
- Raise and lower terrain levels, water depth and bridge heights
- Built-in validation to keep maps compatible with MakaMek
- Save and load maps in MakaMek-compatible formats
- Export maps as PDF
- Free and open source, no ads, no tracking
